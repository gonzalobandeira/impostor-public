Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: March 17, 2026  · 

TL;DR — Who's the Impostor? collects no personal data whatsoever. The game is fully offline and requires no account, no internet connection, and no permissions beyond standard app storage.

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Who's the Impostor? ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our iOS application. We are committed to your privacy: the App is designed to function entirely offline without collecting, transmitting, or storing any personal information.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. The App does not collect, process, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any other identifying information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage statistics or analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Payment information
  • Any other personal or sensitive data

Local Storage

The App saves your preferences (such as selected theme and language) locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults mechanism. This data:

  • Stays entirely on your device
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App

No Internet Connection Required

Who's the Impostor? is a fully offline local-multiplayer game. The App does not require an internet connection and makes no network requests of any kind. There are no servers, no cloud sync, and no remote services.

No Third-Party SDKs

The App does not integrate any third-party libraries, SDKs, or services that could collect data. This includes:

  • No analytics platforms (e.g., Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude)
  • No crash reporting tools (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry)
  • No advertising networks
  • No social media integrations

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, there are no special considerations for users under the age of 13 (or applicable local age threshold). No data from any user, including children, is ever collected or shared.

Changes to This Policy

If our privacy practices ever change, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Given the nature of the App (fully offline, no data collection), we do not anticipate material changes to this policy.
